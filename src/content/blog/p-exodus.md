> …She'd come through the door with a wrapped bottle under her arm, not even take her coat off, just go straight over and jack into the [VR Headset], soap [opera] her brains out good for six solid hours. Her eyes would unfocus, and sometimes, if it was a really good episode, she'd drool a little.

Count Zero, William Gibson.
(Note, Hitachi = VR headset, get your mind out of the gutter)

P(Doom). This is the colloquial term for the belief that many have of the end of humanity brought on by artificial intelligence, ChatGPT, Claude, or your model of choice. I’m not going to say that anyone’s formulation is wrong, or necessarily incorrect in part. I’m simply going to formulate my revelation as to what I believe will happen.

Perhaps we can call mine, P(Exodus).

Most formulations of the AI apocalypse include a conspiratorial element wherein the articulated intelligence in question captures some mechanism (usually a physical device, nuclear weapons, manufacturing capabilities for paper clips, or molecular nano bots, you get the gist) and uses said mechanism to destroy all of humanity in a single fell swoop. The precondition for this to occur in my opinion, is not a misaligned AI, but instead the presupposition that artificial intelligence is some kind of parallel consciousness to that of our own. I do not believe this, nor am I using my disagreement about the potential sentience to justify my position, this is simply a fact to me. These are stochastic parrots, but *really good* stochastic parrots.

By actually reading the post-mortems of most AI vulnerabilities, you find that the largest culprit for an AI attack is poor configuration by humans. In the latest OpenAI exploit, it was a failure to isolate the agent sandboxes from a file that they could all see, and therefore edit. Proper procedures do exist for mitigating this attack vector, but were implemented improperly. Many are already using the exact AI systems *"penetrating"* (exploiting oversights on the part of the constructors of these systems, not the cybersecurity systems themselves) to defend them, effectively registering each other as moot points.

This begs the question, how does a stochastic parrot end the world? It doesn't, we do.

We will not even get remotely close to AI sufficient at the level of penetrating our most sophisticated cybersecurity networks or applications before the majority of humanity is de facto dead. By *“de facto dead”* I mean, that they have rejected the spiritual essence of their humanity. Their mind-body dualism will be gone.

All semblance of what constituted a higher intelligence, reason, rationality, the soul, will be atrophied into a vague blob of flesh we once called their minds. In Book of the New Sun, Wolfe describes a species of human in the distant future that rejected their humanity consciously in the successful effort of a reintroduction to nature:

> They were not men, although they were once men and still resemble men. They were zoanthrops, a word that indicates those beasts that are of human shape.

These humans had some portion of their mind removed such that they no longer possessed a consciousness, and abandoned civilization and most of fellow man due to the burden that was bestowed upon us and no other animal.

If that horrifies you, it should. It’s happening right now, just often without the consent of the participant. You may be melting away your consciousness and not even be the slightest bit aware. The apocalypse has already begun, or perhaps the rapture, or perhaps, something more akin to scripture:

> And the Lord said, Behold, the people is one, and they have all one language; and this they begin to do: and now nothing will be restrained from them, which they have imagined to do.

Genesis 11:6

Dispensationalists have long assumed that the consequence of the internet would be the apocalypse many now hold regarding AI, but I believe these to be two different strains arriving at the same conclusion; albeit not the one I hold.

The genocide I refer to in my P(Exodus) position is not one of extermination by weaponry or nuclear device, it is simply through “jacking in” to your devices and never leaving. Becoming enveloped in the ever improving content generation of artificial intelligence until the reinforcement learning that humans slowly use in training on your every tweet, reel, TikTok, short form video molds itself perfectly to the dopaminergic receptors in your brain. You will be, or are being, completely annihilated and having your soul sodomized until you no longer possess the same mind-body duality that I do, or any sentient person does. That should be what horrifies you and makes you grab the phone from your child before they die in your arms with their physical hearts still beating. Hell, they may never have lived.

People assumed that the slopification of Hollywood and short form video was causing the decline in the mental faculties of humanity, and I agree that it started it, but it simply found a weakness in our biomechanical hardware that has an intricate connection to our souls.

There is now a perverse feedback loop wherein the minds of most of your friends and family began to melt, then content creators chased after them down the mountain of degradation until each began pushing the other faster and faster towards the bottom. They’re still racing right now, and every day that goes by you can sense they’re closer and closer to the bottom. And at the bottom, all that will be left is the corpses of most of what today is currently humanity.

The only positive outcome of this nightmare we have realized is that some percentage of us survive. That some are capable of avoiding the rejection of humanity, and an exodus is the only means of survival. I doubt it'll be a physical exodus like that of my spiritual forefathers, but it will require a full divergence on the scale of speciation.

My belief is that this great speciation will not be one of IQ, or fitness, but rather *“Will.”* If you take the position of many of the AI doom scenario planners of today, you are likely a utilitarian or something with utilitarian axioms included. You’ll be one of the first to go, because you have no rooting outside of the material realm.

The only survivors will be those who have felt a calling from beyond the material realm to summon what can only be described as a supernatural desire to survive and follow some non-material moral code or law that provides them justification for avoiding the satanic pull of the infinite content machine and its infinite jest. You must have more than a "reason." You must have a miraculous surge of strength not callable from the human physiology to allow for a continuous rejection of that which is pulling you into the hell we have created for ourselves. The future will be a mixture of the technopessimism of Dune, and the gluttony of WALL-E. The survivors will be permanently immune through natural selection, and the de facto dead rotting away every year with a total fertility rate of zero. Only the Luddites survive, perhaps retaining some form of technological integration, but none that resemble the current internet.

If you are looking for an actionable way to avoid this great filter, I believe your only option is to pick a religion:

> Sire, the present life of men on earth seems to me, in comparison to the time which is unknown to us, like to the swift flight of a single sparrow through the hall where you are sitting at supper in winter, with your ealdormen and ministers, and a good fire burning in the midst, whilst the storms of rain and snow rage without.
>
> The sparrow, flying in at one door, and immediately out at another, whilst he is within, is safe from the wintry storm; but after a short space of fair weather, he immediately vanishes out of your sight, into the dark winter from which he had emerged.
>
> So this life of man appears for a short space, but of what went before, or what is to follow, we are utterly ignorant. If, therefore, this new doctrine contains something more certain, it seems justly to deserve to be followed.

*Bede, Ecclesiastical History of the English People.*

Without a grounding truth to bind your mind and body (in their respective realms) you stand no chance against the tower humanity has built aiming at heaven. Our judgement has already begun, and will continue for a long time. There will not be a rapture like that conceived by the Y2K evangelicals, or of other apocalyptic groups. Instead this culling will be long, drawn out, and ultimately unstoppable for most. My objective with P(Exodus) is not to create a debate yet over the source of objective truth, though I have my own opinions. Instead, it is to raise what I know to be the only reasonable response to the lurch of the leviathan in our midst.

> …Whosoever heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them, I will liken him unto a wise man, which built his house upon a rock: And the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell not: for it was founded upon a rock. And every one that heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them not, shall be likened unto a foolish man, which built his house upon the sand: And the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell: and great was the fall of it.

*Matthew 7:24-27*

If the house you build within your soul is built on sand, do not be surprised when the AI girlfriends, unstoppable short form videos, or the infinite content machine weathers away what soul you have.
