> …She'd come through the door with a wrapped bottle under her arm, not even take her coat off, just go straight over and jack into the [VR Headset], soap [opera] her brains out good for six solid hours. Her eyes would unfocus, and sometimes, if it was a really good episode, she'd drool a little.

Count Zero, William Gibson.
(Note, Hitachi = VR headset, get your mind out of the gutter)

P(Doom). This is the colloquial term for the belief that many have of the end of humanity brought on by artificial intelligence, ChatGPT, Claude, or your model of choice. I’m not going to say that anyone’s formulation is wrong, or necessarily incorrect in part. I’m simply going to formulate my revelation as to what I believe will happen.

Perhaps we can call mine, P(Exodus).

Most formulations of the AI apocalypse include a conspiratorial element wherein the articulated intelligence in question capture control of some mechanism (usually a physical device, nuclear weapons, manufacturing capabilities for paper clips, or molecular nano bots, you get the gist) and using said control to annihilate all of humanity in a single fell swoop. The precondition for this to occur in my opinion, is not a misaligned AI, but instead the presupposition that artificial intelligence is some kind of parallel consciousness to that of our own. I do not believe this, nor am I using my disagreement of the sentient or non sentience to justify my position, this is simply a fact to me. These are stochastic parrots, but *really good* stochastic parrots.

By actually reading the post mortems of most AI vulnerability post-mortems, you can find that the largest culprit for an AI attack is poor configuration by humans. In the case of the latest OpenAI exploit, a failure to completely isolate the agent sandboxes from accessing a file that they can all see, and therefore edit. Proper procedures do exist that would completely annihilate any possibility of a human making this mistake, and many are already using the exact AI systems *"penetrating"* (exploiting oversights on the point of the constructors of these systems, not the cybersecurity systems themselves) to defend them, effectively registering each other as moot points.

This begs the question, how does a stochastic parrot end the world? It doesnt, we do.

We will not even get remotely close to AI sufficient at the level of penetrating our most sophisticated cybersecurity networks or applications before the majority of humanity is defacto dead. By *“defacto dead”* I mean, rejected their humanity entirely, the spiritual essence of humanity. Their mind-body dualism will be gone.

All semblance of what constituted a higher intelligence, reason, rationality, the soul, will be atrophied into a vague blob of flesh we once called their minds. In Book of the New Sun, Wolfe describes a species of human in the distant future that rejected their humanity conciously in the successful effort of a reintroduction to nature:

> They were not men, although they were once men and still resemble men. They were zoanthrops, a word that indicates those beasts that are of human shape.

These humans had some portion of their mind removed such that they no longer possessed a consciousness, and abandoned civilization and most of fellow man due to the burden that was bestowed upon us and no other animal.

If that horrifies you, it should. It’s happening right now, just often without the consent of the participant. You may be melting away your consciousness and not even be the slightest aware. The apocalypse has already begun, or perhaps the rapture, or perhaps, something more akin to scripture:

> And the Lord said, Behold, the people is one, and they have all one language; and this they begin to do: and now nothing will be restrained from them, which they have imagined to do.

Genesis 11:6

Dispensationalists have long assumed that the consequence of the internet would be the apocalypse many now hold regarding AI, but I believe these to be two different strains arriving at the same conclusion; albeit not the one I hold.

The genocide I refer to in my P(Exodus) position is not one of annihilation by weaponry or nuclear device, it is simply through “jacking in” to your devices and never leaving. Becoming enveloped in the ever improving content generation of artificial intelligence until the reinforcement learning that humans slowly use in training on your every tweet, reel, tiktok, short form video. You will, or are being completely annihilated and having your soul sodomized until you no longer possess the same mind-body duality that I do, or any sentient person does. That should be what horrifies you and makes you grab the phone from your child before they die in your arms with their physical hearts still beating. Hell, they may never have lived.

People assumed that the slopification of Hollywood and short form video was causing the decline in the mental facilities of humanity, and I agree that it started it, but it simply found a weakness in our biomechanical hardware that has an intricate connection to our souls.

There is now a perverse feedback loop wherein the minds of most of your friends and family began to melt, then content creators chased after them down the mountain of degradation until each began pushing the other faster and faster towards the bottom. They’re still racing right now, and every day that goes by you can sense they’re closer and closer to the bottom. And at the bottom, all that will be left is the corpses of most of what today is currently humanity.

So that leads to the only positive outcome, that some percentage of us survive. That some are capable of avoiding the rejection of humanity, and an exodus is the only means of survival. I doubt it'll be a physical edoxus like that of my spiritual forefathers, but it will require a full divergence on the scale of speciation.

My belief is that this great speciation will not be one of IQ, or fitness, but rather *“Will.”* If you take the position of many of the AI doom scenario planners of today, you are likely a utilitarian or something with utilitarian axioms included. You’ll be one of the first to go, because you have no rooting outside of the material realm.

The only survivors will be those who have felt a calling from beyond the material realm to summon what can only be described as a supernatural desire to survive and follow some non-material moral code or law that provides them justification for avoiding the satanic pull of the infinite content machine and its infinite jest. You must have more than simply a "reason" you must have a miraculous surge of strength not callable from the human physiology to allow for a continuous rejection of that which is pulling you into the hell we have created for ourselves.

You better pick a religion, fast. And make sure it’s the right one. It can’t just be a surface level association like many mainline denominations today, or else you may as well be holding on to a cliff face coated in Vaseline. Your faith has to be so robust and deep rooted within your soul that you can earnestly look into the abyss of infinitely attractive food that causes you to drool in the real world, video games so fun you physically cannot stop playing, shows so thrilling they have until you die of starvation you’ll be sitting on the edge of your seat; and most of all, girlfriends and boyfriends, potentially spouses, that can love you so perfectly, so carefully, that you no longer desire to mate. Why would you after all? Your AI girlfriend won’t ever let you down.
